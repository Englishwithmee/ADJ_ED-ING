ED or ING?
Adjective Masterclass · University English A1–A2
Part 1 · Understand the difference

How do you feel?
What makes you feel that way?

Use -ed adjectives to talk about feelings and -ing adjectives to describe their causes.

Feelings

-ED adjectives

Describe how a person feels.

“I am bored.”
“She is excited about the trip.”
“He feels embarrassed.”
“We were surprised by the news.”

Causes

-ING adjectives

Describe something or someone that causes a feeling.

“The lesson is boring.”
“The trip is exciting.”
“The situation is embarrassing.”
“The news was surprising.”

Compare them

-ED · Feeling-ING · Cause
I am bored.The film is boring.
She feels interested.The book is interesting.
We are excited.The match is exciting.
He was confused.The instructions were confusing.
I am tired.The work is tiring.
Remember: -ED = how someone feels.   -ING = what causes the feeling. People can also be interesting, annoying or inspiring when they cause that feeling.
